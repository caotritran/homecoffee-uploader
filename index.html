HomeCoffee Uploader

A personal upload tool for the HomeCoffee TikTok channel

HomeCoffee Uploader is a private, single-user tool. The channel owner uses it to send videos they edited themselves to their own TikTok account as drafts. The owner then finishes and publishes each draft manually in the TikTok app.

What it does

  • Signs in with TikTok (Login Kit) to get the owner's permission.
  • Uploads a finished video file to the owner's TikTok inbox/drafts through the Content Posting API (video.upload).
  • Never publishes on its own. Every post is reviewed and published by the owner in the TikTok app.

Who can use it

Only the channel owner. It is not offered to the public and has no other users.
